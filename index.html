Findings below reflect my observations during independent audits using public DNS records, live on-site testing, and triggered email flow observation. No internal system access was used.

Full written reports include 10 to 15 findings, screenshots, fix priorities, and technical detail. Request a report ↗

01

MOSH

moshlife.com ↗

Functional food / Brain health · $3M seed

Shopify / Klaviyo / SendGrid / Mailgun

Critical

DMARC policy set to p=none

No DMARC enforcement was requested by the domain policy, leaving the domain without policy-based quarantine or rejection of unauthenticated impersonation attempts.

High

Account and newsletter profiles remained separate

Live observation showed browse-abandonment firing to a separate profile for the same person. The audit identified a profile-stitching gap affecting account-created customers across browse, cart, checkout, and post-purchase automation.

High

Three active ESPs, one authorized in SPF

The audit identified Klaviyo, SendGrid, and Mailgun in the sending stack, with only one represented in SPF. Klaviyo and SendGrid authentication paths were flagged for review because of potential SPF failures and inbox-placement impact.

02

Jolie

jolieskinco.com ↗

Home wellness / Filtered showerheads · $4M seed

Shopify / Klaviyo / SendGrid / Recharge

Critical

Footer signup returned errors on every tested submission

Repeated testing across browsers and email addresses found the brand’s proactive email capture form failing. Visitors attempting to subscribe through this channel were unable to join the list.

High

Transactional mail used Shopify’s generic sending domain

The audit observed separate sender identities for transactional and marketing mail. Transactional volume was not contributing to the brand’s own sending-domain reputation.

High

DMARC enforcement limited to pct=50

The published policy applied quarantine to only 50% of messages failing DMARC, leaving enforcement partial.

03

Women’s health DTC brand

Brand anonymized

Supplements / Vitamins · Seed-funded

Klaviyo / Octane AI / Rebuy / Shopify

High

Lifecycle quiz answers did not personalize the welcome flow

The Octane AI quiz captured TTC, postpartum, and perimenopause stages, but the observed welcome email was generic regardless of the answer. Stage-specific positioning was not reflected in the inbox experience.

High

Quiz email did not carry through to checkout

Visitors were asked to re-enter the email already captured by the quiz. The audit identified an identity-stitching gap between Octane AI and Shopify that limited recovery of pre-checkout abandonment.

Critical

DMARC policy set to p=none

A brand positioned around OB/GYN medical credibility had no policy-based DMARC enforcement, creating an impersonation risk for a health-authority identity.
